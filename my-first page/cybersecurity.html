Cybersecurity

Small habits can make a big difference in keeping your accounts and devices safer online.

Start with the basics

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, accounts, and information from online threats. These are useful places to begin:

  • Use a unique password for each account, and turn on multi-factor authentication.
  • Pause before opening unexpected links or attachments.
  • Install updates for your devices and apps.
